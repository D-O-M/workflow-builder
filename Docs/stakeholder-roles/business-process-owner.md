# UP-01 Business process owner

## Perspective

The owner defines what one or more business processes must achieve and gives the Blueprint author the requirements for a change. This profile also includes business-facing staff who need to explain an individual outcome, such as a call-centre employee looking up an account.

## Working knowledge

- They know the **end-to-end business process**, from the event that starts it to the people who rely on its result. They understand the consequences of delay or partial completion.
- They know **why requirements vary** across customers and markets, which commitments apply to everyone and which exceptions the business has deliberately agreed.
- They recognise **where change becomes expensive**. Even a small revision can mean coordinating with downstream teams or supporting old and new commitments simultaneously.
- They interpret **outcomes at two scales**. An account history can explain one missed promise. A pattern across accounts can change which problems the owner prioritises.

## What guides their decisions

A Process can finish successfully after the customer's deadline. Owners judge a change by whether it improves the business outcome that prompted it. [APQC guidance on business process ownership](https://www.apqc.org/resources/blog/business-process-owners)

For a new customer, owners want to be able to point to what changes in the sequence and why. Sharing the common tasks keeps the next change manageable.

## What they need

- A versioned Blueprint they can rely on to follow the agreed sequence each time, and can ask the author to change as requirements evolve.
- Success and failure information across their Processes, with an exact history of Steps and interventions for a particular account or case.
- A way to prioritise urgent Processes and issues, and confidence that partly completed work can be retried or resumed.

# UP-03 Process operations team

## Perspective

Process operators monitor business work in progress, investigate affected cases and explain the impact to the people relying on it. Their technical experience varies.

## Working knowledge

- They recognise the **normal rhythm of the work**. Changes in volume, waiting times and failure patterns help them distinguish a busy period from a growing incident.
- They judge **impact and urgency** from the cases affected. One blocked case with an imminent deadline can take priority over a larger group that can wait.
- They understand **what recovery will repeat**. Prior actions, stale inputs and uncertain outcomes determine which Steps are safe to repeat and which cases can be recovered together.
- They can **reconstruct and communicate events**, using Process history and recorded interventions to give stakeholders a timely account and developers enough evidence to investigate what remains unexplained.

## What guides their decisions

Business impact and urgency guide which issues they tackle first. If an external dependency returns after an outage, an operator still needs to establish which affected Processes have recovered and which require attention. That distinction matters when giving internal and external stakeholders an early update. [SFIA incident management](https://sfia-online.org/en/sfia-9/skills/incident-management)

An operator may need to refresh an earlier Step's data while skipping a later one that already sent the customer an email.

## What they need

- An overview of Process health and errors, with advanced search and filtering to find related cases and inspect individual Steps.
- Metrics, logs and telemetry that support proactive alerts and help establish the extent of a problem.
- Control over the priority of urgent Processes and issues.
- Individual and bulk recovery, with a visual preview of the chosen continuation point and the Steps to repeat or exclude.
- An exact history of Process activity and human interventions for investigation, reporting and audit.

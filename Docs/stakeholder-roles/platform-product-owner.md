# UP-07 Platform product owner

## Perspective

The platform product owner sets WorkflowBuilder's direction across the business and considers its potential for sale to other organisations. Their view spans the teams that use, develop and operate it.

## Working knowledge

- They understand **business needs across teams**. Different terminology can hide a common requirement, while apparently similar requests can depend on genuinely different business rules.
- They know **what drives adoption**, from finding and trying a capability to running it independently. Repeated requests for specialist help can explain why a team stops using it.
- They weigh **value and cost across the product's life**, including delivery effort, ongoing support and work a change might shift from authors to operators.
- They understand **what makes a commercial opportunity credible**. Internal reuse demonstrates value across teams. A wider market depends on how other organisations work and what would make switching worthwhile.

## What guides their decisions

One team's urgent request may reveal a shared need or an exception best handled in its Blueprint. Before investing in a platform-wide change, the owner wants to know who else would benefit and how they would tell it helped. [Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework – Product manager](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/role/product-manager/)

One sign of value is that a second team can discover a capability and use it for its own work. The owner wants to understand what made adoption worthwhile for that team and where it still needed help. [CNCF platform engineering maturity model](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platform-eng-maturity-model/)

## What they need

- A generic platform on which teams can meet different requirements through combinations of reusable tasks.
- Evidence from authors, operators and developers about the work the platform saves and where it still causes difficulty.
- Insight into whether other organisations face similar problems, so they can judge the potential for selling WorkflowBuilder to third parties.

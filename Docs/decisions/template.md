# NNNN · Decision title

- **Status** — Proposed, accepted, superseded or withdrawn
- **Date** — YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision makers** — People or team making the choice

## Context

What constraint or problem requires a decision? Link to the evidence and state any assumptions.

## Decision

Describe the chosen approach, the alternatives considered and why the choice fits.

## Consequences

State who must change what, the trade-offs and when to revisit the decision. Link to implementation, verification and any record this decision supersedes.
